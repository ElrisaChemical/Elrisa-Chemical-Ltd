Flexible foam

Soft, springs back

Open-cell foam for mattresses, furniture, car seats and packaging. Made from long-chain polyols (molecular weight 3,000–8,000) with TDI or MDI, usually blown with water.

Rigid foam

Hard, insulating

Closed-cell foam for panels, cold rooms, refrigerators, pipes and spray insulation. Made from short-chain, high-functionality polyols with polymeric MDI. One of the best insulators available.

Semi-rigid & CASE

Tough and elastic

Shoe soles, rollers, wheels, sealants, adhesives and coatings use diols and triols with modified MDI or TDI prepolymers and curing agents.

Polyether polyol

The all-rounder

Made from propylene and ethylene oxide on a starter such as glycerine, sucrose or sorbitol. Used in almost all flexible foam and as the backbone of rigid foam. Hydroxyl value sets how hard or soft the foam is.

Polyester polyol

Fire and stability

Aromatic polyesters improve flame resistance, char and dimensional stability in rigid foam, which is why spray and panel systems use them. Often blended with polyether polyol.

Polymer polyol

Firmness without weight

Polyether polyol carrying fine SAN particles. Raises the hardness and load-bearing of flexible foam, so you reach the firmness you want at a lower density.

FAQ

Polyol and PU foam questions

What chemicals do I need to make polyurethane foam?

Polyurethane foam is made by mixing two liquids: a polyol side and an isocyanate side. For flexible foam such as mattresses you need a flexible polyether polyol (often with a polymer polyol for hardness), TDI or MDI, water as the blowing agent, amine and tin catalysts, a silicone surfactant and optionally methylene chloride, flame retardant and colour. For rigid insulation foam most producers buy a ready-made blend polyol system and react it with polymeric MDI. Elrisa supplies all of these and can work out the exact recipe with you.

How do I start a foam mattress factory?

Start by deciding the foam grades and daily output you want, then choose a production method (box foaming for small starts, a continuous slabstock line for larger volumes), the cutting equipment and a reliable supply of polyol, TDI and additives. Elrisa helps new foam makers through each step: formulation, raw material lists and costs, equipment advice, trial batches in our laboratory, staff training and ongoing supply. Fill in the foam production form and we will contact you.

What is the difference between polyether and polyester polyol?

Polyether polyols are made from propylene and ethylene oxide on a starter such as glycerine or sucrose. They are used in almost all flexible foam and in many rigid foams. Aromatic polyester polyols are made from acids and glycols. They are used in rigid insulation foam because they improve flame resistance and dimensional stability. Many rigid foam formulas blend the two.

What is a blend polyol or PU foam system?

A blend polyol, also called a system or component A, is a ready-mixed polyol side that already contains the catalysts, surfactant, flame retardant and blowing agent. You only add the isocyanate (component B, usually polymeric MDI) in the stated ratio. Systems make rigid foam production simpler and more consistent, which is why spray, panel and appliance producers use them.

What is the difference between rigid and flexible polyurethane foam?

Flexible foam is soft and springs back. It is used in mattresses, furniture, car seats and packaging, and is made from long-chain polyols (molecular weight 3,000 to 8,000) with TDI or MDI. Rigid foam is hard, closed-cell and insulating. It is used in panels, cold rooms, refrigerators, pipes and spray insulation, and is made from short-chain, high-functionality polyols with polymeric MDI.

How are polyols and isocyanates packed and stored?

Polyols and isocyanates are shipped in 200 to 250 kg steel drums or 1,000 to 1,250 kg IBC tanks, and in bulk for large users. Keep them sealed, dry and out of direct sun. Isocyanates react with moisture, so close containers straight after use and follow the Safety Data Sheet. We send the TDS and SDS with every order.

Is HCFC-141b still allowed for PU foam?

HCFC-141b is being phased out worldwide under the Montreal Protocol, and many countries already ban or restrict it in foam. Elrisa offers water-blown, pentane, cyclopentane and HFC-245fa systems, and will advise on the rules in your country before you choose a system.
